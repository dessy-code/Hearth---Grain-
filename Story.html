Story & Craft

We make fewer things,
and we make them to stay.

Furniture should get better with the years, and be mended instead of thrown away. Here is how we build, what we use, and who is behind it.

Why we started

Furniture that gives up
after two winters.

Our founder spent years furnishing homes with pieces that looked good in a photograph and fell apart in real life. Loose joints. Fabric that pilled. Wood that was veneer over particleboard, dressed up as “solid.”

Hearth & Grain began with a simple promise: build the kind of furniture people keep, and tell the truth about how it is made. No vague claims about “sustainability.” No “artisan-inspired” language. Just the workshop, the timber, the joinery, and the people who put it together.

— Alex Rivera, founder

From timber to your front door

Every piece passes through
fourteen pairs of hands.

01

Design & timber selection

Every piece starts as a drawing. Then we choose the boards: walnut for warmth, white oak for strength, smoked oak for depth. We reject anything with checks, knots in the wrong place, or grain that will fight the joinery.

02

Joinery

Frames are joined with mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joints—not staples and glue alone. It takes longer. It also means the frame will not rack or wobble after a year of daily use. The maker’s initials are stamped inside every frame.

03

Upholstery

Eight-way hand-tied springs. Down-wrapped cushions or high-resilience foam. Covers are cut, sewn, and fitted by hand. If you order a washable cover, we sew it so it can come off and go back on without a fight.

04

Finishing & quality check

Hand-sanded to 220 grit. Finished with a hardwax oil that protects without hiding the grain. Then it sits under natural light for a day. If the finish is uneven, it goes back. If the joints are tight and the grain sings, it goes to you.

Honest about what we do,
and what we have not solved yet

Four commitments.
Evidence attached.

Responsibly sourced timber

FSC-certified or reclaimed. We name the mill and the forest region on every product page. Certificates are linked, not implied.

Made to order

We do not build for a warehouse. Your piece is cut, joined, and upholstered after you order it. Less waste, less storage, no landfill of unsold sofas.

Reclaimed & offcut wood

Offcuts become stools, side tables, and drawer pulls. Reclaimed timber is used wherever the grain is good enough. We do not hide the patches.

Packaging that can be reused

Felt blankets and cardboard corners. No polystyrene. The delivery team takes the blankets back if you do not want them.

We publish our certificates and our targets, and we update them once a year. If a claim cannot be verified, we remove it.
