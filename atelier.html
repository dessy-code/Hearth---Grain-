Atelier FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Most custom items are non-returnable. That is why we send free swatches and offer a design consult before you commit. If something arrives damaged or not as specified, we fix it. Read the full policy on the Visit & Support page.

Yes, within 24 hours of ordering. After that, the timber may already be cut. Email us and we will tell you exactly where your piece is in the build.

Removable covers can be machine washed cold on a gentle cycle. Care guidance is on each product page. Bouclé should be professionally cleaned. We include a care card with every swatch kit.
