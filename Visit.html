What is not returnable

  • Custom or made-to-order pieces (non-returnable unless defective)
  • Clearance or final-sale items
  • Items damaged by misuse or improper assembly
  • Natural variations in wood grain or stone (not defects)

If something arrives damaged or not as specified, we fix it. Report within 48 hours with photos. Read the full policy on request—or ask us to email it.

Care & repair

Keep it going.

Spare covers. Replaceable cushions. Refinishing for wood. The piece is not finished when it leaves the workshop.

Wood

Re-oil every 6–12 months. Wipe with a damp cloth, avoid harsh chemicals.

Read guide →

Fabric

Vacuum weekly with a soft brush. Removable covers machine wash cold.

Read guide →

Stone

Wipe spills immediately. Use a pH-neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic products.

Read guide →

Metal

Chrome: wipe with a soft cloth. Brass: polish with a specialist pad to restore finish.
